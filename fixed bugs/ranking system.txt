Ranking logic :


Rating Score: Measures the quality of past consultations based on client star feedback.

Completed Sessions: Measures platform activity and popularity via total successful sessions.

Professional Experience: Measures industry authority based on years of prior work history.

Response Rate: Measures expert reliability and speed in accepting or declining client requests.

Profile Freshness: Measures recent platform engagement and recency of expert activity.

Repeat Bookings: Measures true expert value based on how many clients return for multiple sessions.


Expert Score = (Average Rating * 20) + (Completed Sessions * 2) + (Experience Years * 4) + (Response Rate * 10) + (Recency Bonus * 10) + (Repeat Bookings * 5)
